# Privacy Policy

Black Box does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- It only runs on x.com / twitter.com. It reads the page layout locally to find empty timeline boxes and,
  if you turn that option on, posts labeled as ads.
- It makes no network requests.
- Its only permission is `storage`, used to save your two on/off settings. If you use browser sync, your
  browser may sync these settings across your own devices. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else.
